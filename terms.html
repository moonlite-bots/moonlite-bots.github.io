Using Kirby Watcher

These terms apply to your use of Kirby Watcher, a companion bot for Kirby bot's collection game. By using Watcher, you agree to these terms and the data practices described in its Privacy Policy.

You must meet Discord's minimum age requirements and follow Discord's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and the rules of the servers where you use Watcher.

What Watcher does

Watcher provides collection tracking, wishlists, personal and server alerts, party assistance, and community leaderboard features. It interprets Kirby bot's announcements and results to help keep your tracking information up to date.

Watcher does not collect Kirbys on your behalf, choose game winners, or change the collection records held by Kirby bot. Watcher's statistics describe the activity it has tracked and may differ from your collection in the game.

Your settings and responsibilities

You are responsible for choosing and reviewing your personal settings and keeping your wishlist information accurate. When Watcher asks you to review a result, check it before confirming an update.

Only configure server settings, channels, or roles when you have permission to do so. Server staff are responsible for choosing suitable alert destinations and informing their community about enabled features, including global feeds that forward game alerts between servers.

Acceptable use

Do not use Watcher to harass others, send spam, bypass access restrictions, exploit bugs, or access another person's private information or settings without permission. Do not interfere with the bot or use it in ways that violate Discord's rules or applicable law.

If you find a bug, security concern, or misuse of the bot, report it through one of the support options below.

Tracking accuracy and availability

Alerts and tracking depend on Discord permissions, Kirby bot's messages, and service availability. Messages may be delayed, missed, or interpreted incorrectly. Features may be changed, interrupted, or discontinued.

Review important collection changes against Kirby bot's own records. Watcher's alerts, statistics, and leaderboard entries are not a guarantee of a collection, trade, game result, or prize entitlement. Any separately announced prize conditions govern eligibility and claims.

Privacy and removing your setup

The Privacy Policy explains what Watcher processes, where information is stored, and how to request access, correction, or deletion.

Pausing alerts keeps your saved setup. Removing all personal setups and saved preferences stops new personal collection tracking. If you already have a score for the current week, your existing statistics remain through that week's leaderboard results and are cleared after the next completed reset. Otherwise, personal tracking data is cleared through Watcher's cleanup process.

You may ask the developer to clear your personal setups through email or a community support ticket. This follows the same weekly-score rule. Previously posted Discord messages and separate server or administrative records are not automatically erased by a personal reset; contact support about those records.
