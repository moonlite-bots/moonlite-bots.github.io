What information Watcher uses

Kirby Watcher is a companion bot for Kirby bot's collection game. It processes game announcements and results from the configured Kirby bot, including Kirby numbers, collection results, relevant reactions, message links, and image links. These events support spawns, parties, KHAOS, Mega KHAOS, Ultimate KHAOS, Mystery Kirby, gives, trades, and battles.

Watcher uses Discord user, server, channel, role, and message IDs. Names, avatars, and role information may be used to display alerts, identify participants, and run community winner features. It does not save a complete directory of server members.

Saved personal information can include Radar and Mystery alert settings, server-specific wishlists, preferred servers, collection activity totals, and leaderboard records. Saved server and administrative information can include alert destinations, role and access settings, developer release records, and access restrictions.

Watcher does not keep an archive of ordinary Discord conversations. Its game tracking reads Kirby bot's messages rather than ordinary conversation text. Ordinary message events may also be used for configured weekly-winner reactions. Temporary tracking records and saved message references help Watcher update alerts, remove old bot messages, and avoid counting the same result twice.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the features users and server staff configure: alerts, wishlist updates, collection tracking, party assistance, community leaderboards, and support. It is also used to maintain access controls and troubleshoot the bot.

Moonlite Bots does not sell bot user data, use it for advertising, or use Discord message content to train machine learning or AI models.

Where information is stored and displayed

The bot runs on the operator's computer. Its saved bot data files remain on that computer and are not backed up or uploaded to an external storage service.

Feature outputs are sent through Discord. Personal alerts appear in DMs; server alerts appear in configured channels. Leaderboards, winner announcements, and collection update messages may be visible to other users. Configured global feeds may forward Kirby event details, image links, and source-message links to other servers. The operator can view information needed to manage the bot and respond to support requests.

If you contact support, the information you provide is handled through Proton Mail or Discord. Email and support-ticket records are separate from the bot's local tracking files. Discord, Proton Mail, GitHub Pages, and linked image services process information under their own policies. The public website contains support and policy information, not users' saved bot tracking records.

Your settings and deletion choices

Use Watcher's dashboards to manage or remove your personal setups. Pausing alerts keeps your saved setup and is different from deleting it. An empty wishlist can still be an active setup, for example to track future releases.

When you no longer have any personal features or saved preferences configured, Watcher stops adding new collection activity to your personal statistics. Your personal tracking data is cleared unless you already have a collection score for the current week. In that case, your existing statistics remain through that week's leaderboard results and are deleted after the next completed leaderboard reset. Cleanup depends on the bot running successfully; interrupted cleanup is retried.

You can also request access, correction, or deletion by emailing moonlitebots@proton.me or opening a community support ticket. Include your Discord user ID and explain your request. We may ask you to confirm ownership of the affected Discord account or your authority to manage the server. Do not send passwords or bot tokens.

The developer can clear your personal setups for you. Once processed, this follows the same weekly-score rule described above. Setting up personal features again starts tracking again.

Deleting personal tracking data does not automatically erase previously posted Discord messages or every server and administrative record. Server configuration does not automatically disappear when Watcher leaves a server. Access restrictions and other records needed to administer the service are handled separately. Contact support if your request concerns those records or an existing message. Rights available under applicable privacy law are not limited by these feature controls.

How long information is kept

Personal setups and associated activity data are kept while they remain configured, subject to the cleanup rules above. Server and administrative records do not have a general automatic expiry period. Support information is kept as needed to handle requests and follow-up. Temporary alerts have different deletion times depending on the feature; deleting an alert is different from deleting saved tracking data.

Changes and questions

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Significant changes will also be announced through the community server and available bot update channels.

For questions about this policy or your information, email moonlitebots@proton.me or use the community support ticket channel linked above.
